01 Terminal

A modern terminal,
as your Android home.

Woven into Android itself. Run the TUI apps you use every day, launch anything from the dock, and answer a notification with a swipe up on its app icon.

Panes
Split, move, resize. Sessions hold windows, windows hold panes. No tmux needed.
Command palette
Every action, one swipe. Swipe up on the space bar and type.
Keyboard
A port of Julow's Unexpected Keyboard. Theme it, set any font, reshape the layout, keep a clipboard inside it.
Touch
Drag a pane's edge to resize it. Swap two panes with a two-finger swipe. Pinch to zoom one pane.
Status bar
Swipe down from the terminal's top edge. Now playing, live system stats through Shizuku, and the notifications you marked essential.
tlstore
Builds patched for the launcher. fastfetch that plays GIFs, and dawn, a notes app that writes, searches and speaks with the models on your phone.
Graphics
Kitty and sixel. Images and previews render inline, in the shell.
Panes and sessions

02 Widgets

Your widgets,
one swipe left.

A page for the widgets you actually need. Eleven first-party ones ship with it, drawn in the launcher's own theme, beside any Android widget.

Get there
Hold the pane border and swipe, or tap the house icon at the status bar's edge.
Built in
Analog and Digital clock, Agenda, Calendar, Weather, Battery, System, Media, Notifications, Tasks, Scratchpad.
Add
Hold the page to add a widget or edit the ones you have.
Edit mode
Hold the pane border for more controls. In edit mode, set the grid's rows and columns.
The Widgets page

03 Display

A third place:
a Linux desktop.

A built-in X11 display, so the full GUI apps in the termux-x11 repository run on the phone too.

Server
Install from the termux-x11 repository and the app lands in the drawer. Launch it like any Android app.
Reach it
Hold the pane border and drag, tap the screen glyph at the status bar's edge, or bind Go to Display.
Controls
Hold the display's border for its controls: change scaling on the fly, or switch the display off to free resources.
Windows
The status bar lists the display's windows. Tap one to switch, tap it again for a close button.
Touchpad
A touchpad mode that behaves like a touch screen. Scroll with one finger, tap a text field and the keyboard rises.
Linux display

04 Look & layout

Glass, Material,
laid out your way.

Four sane defaults, or tune every element yourself.

Look
Clear, Mist, Tint, Solid, Custom. At Custom: blur, grain, opacity, tint, margin and corner radius, per surface.
Clock
Tap the status bar to tune it on its own, with faces Flip, LCD, Minimal, LED matrix, Tape, Slab.
Layout
Docked or floating. Corners, margin, hidden elements. Portrait and landscape each keep their own.
Wallpaper
Set it from Appearance and the glass gains AGSL-shaded effects and a parallax scroll under the wallpaper.
Icons
System icons or any installed icon pack, for the pinned row only if you prefer.
Effects
Cursor trails and terminal effects, a contrast control for the terminal, size and icons for the dock.
Look and themes

05 On-device AI

Models that
live on the phone.

Speech in, speech out, an assistant, a search index. Even the app drawer is sorted by a model that never leaves the phone.

Drawer
Categories, sorted on the phone. Gemma groups your installed apps into Communication, Work, Photo, Desktops, Linux apps and more. Nothing leaves the device.
Runtimes
LiteRT-LM by Google and MNN-LLM by Alibaba. CPU or GPU, picked per function.
Endpoint
OpenAI and Ollama compatible APIs on localhost. Existing SDKs and tools just work.
Speech
Whisper and Parakeet for voice typing, and Gemma tidies what you said. KittenTTS reads aloud.
Modalities
Text, vision and audio input. Embeddings for search by meaning. Image generation.
Model centre
One row per job: Automatic, On this phone, Remote or Off. Benchmark the models to find what suits your phone.
On-device AI
